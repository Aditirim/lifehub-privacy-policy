# Privacy Policy for LifeHub

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

LifeHub ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal productivity application developed and published by Duodevs.

This Privacy Policy explains how LifeHub collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the LifeHub mobile application.

By using LifeHub, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, LifeHub may collect or process the following information:

### Account Information

If you create or sign in to a LifeHub account, we may process information associated with your account, such as:

- Name
- Email address
- User account identifier
- Authentication information provided through supported sign-in methods

### User-Created Content

LifeHub may store information that you voluntarily enter into the app, such as:

- Tasks
- Notes
- Habits
- Reminders
- Calendar-related information
- Other productivity information you choose to save

### Location Information

If you use a feature that requires your device location, LifeHub may request access to location information.

Location information is used only to provide features that require location access. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

### Device and Technical Information

LifeHub and the third-party services used by the app may process technical information such as:

- Device type
- Operating system
- App version
- Device identifiers
- IP address
- Diagnostic and technical information

This information may be used to maintain, secure, and improve the application.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by LifeHub may be used to:

- Provide and operate the app's features
- Authenticate users
- Store and synchronize user data
- Provide reminders, notifications, alarms, and other requested functionality
- Provide weather-related features when used
- Maintain application security
- Diagnose technical problems
- Improve application reliability and functionality
- Respond to user support requests

We do not sell your personal information.
