# Delete Your LifeHub Account

## Request Account Deletion

If you want to delete your LifeHub account and associated data, you can request deletion using the contact information below.

### How to Request Deletion

Send an email to:

**duodevs.tech@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**LifeHub Account Deletion Request**

Please send the request from the email address associated with your LifeHub account.

Include:

- Your LifeHub account email address
- A request stating that you want your LifeHub account deleted

For security purposes, we may need to verify that the request was made by the account owner.

## What Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete your LifeHub account and associated user data, including:

- Firebase Authentication account
- Notes
- Habits
- Events
- Transactions
- Other personal data associated with your LifeHub user account

## Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law, for security purposes, or where necessary to resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations.

Any retained information will be kept only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose.

## In-App Account Deletion

LifeHub also provides an account deletion option directly inside the app.

You can find it at:

**Profile → Delete Account**

Using the in-app option allows you to directly delete your account and associated user data.
